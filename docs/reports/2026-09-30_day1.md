Read-outs:
1. **Movement is large compared with the spread.** Unlike SideHustle's BANKNIFTY finding, the spread is *not* the main obstacle on Nifty. Fixed brokerage and taxes probably are, so `costs.yaml` is urgent.
2. **Random entries with a fixed time exit lose, even before charges.** Any edge has to come from **direction and exit management**, not from the market simply moving.
3. With perfect hindsight about direction, the median MFE doubles (+₹1,229 at 25 min). That gap is the whole game, and it is exactly what the baselines and the agent have to prove they can capture.
4. MAE at 25 min is about 13 premium pts (median), and 19 pts at the 75th percentile. A ₹2,000 per-trade risk equals about 30 pts on 1 lot, or 15 pts on 2 lots. **With 2 lots, a 15-pt stop sits inside ordinary noise.**
