# Exit variants X1 / X2 for six candidates (PRE-DECLARED 2026-10-04, before any code or result)

**Basis:** the exit diagnosis found that holding to 15:10 is right, and the only "room" is **give-back** (trades well in profit that end as losers) in G1, P4, CPR, R6, SW1 and SW2. So both variants act **only after a large gain**. Until then, every trade runs exactly as its frozen rule says: same entry, same stop, same thesis exit, same time exit.

## The variants (identical definitions for every candidate of a type; no tuning)

| Type | X1: breakeven lock | X2: keep half the gain |
|---|---|---|
| **Long option** (G1, P4, CPR) | Once the premium's 1-min **close** has reached **+50%** over the entry price, the stop moves to the **entry price** (tighten only). | Once a close reaches **+50%**, the stop moves to **entry + 50% of (best close − entry)**, updated as the best close rises. |
| **Sold straddle** (R6) | Once the straddle's value (sum of 1-min closes) has fallen to **≤ 50% of the credit**, buy back at the next open if the value returns to **≥ the credit**. | Once value ≤ 50% of the credit, buy back at the next open if value rises to **≥ lowest value + 50% of (credit − lowest value)**. |
| **Swing stock** (SW1, SW2) | Once a daily **close** is **≥ +3%** over entry, sell at the next open if a later close is **< entry**. | Once a close is **≥ +5%**, sell at the next open if a later close is **< entry + 50% of (best close − entry)**. |

**Common to both variants:**
- The original stop, thesis exit and time exit all stay in force.
- Option stops fill pessimistically, as in the original simulator: the minute's low against the stop, filled at min(open, stop).
- Same costs.

## Historical check (a filter only, not a selection)
- **Trades:** each candidate's own historical trades, Dec 2023 – Sep 2026, generated by the frozen signal code. The base and the variants use the **same signals**.
- **Two halves:** split at the median trade date per candidate.
- **A variant goes forward unless it is worse than the base in BOTH halves.** "Worse" = lower mean net per trade. A variant may also go forward when it is better in only one half, because the forward data decides.
- **Reported:** mean net per trade (base / X1 / X2) per half and overall; how often each variant changed the outcome; the average gain on "rescued" trades vs the average loss on "cut" winners.

## Forward
Surviving variants are added to the forward tournament as extra rows (e.g. `G1-X1`), scored each evening on the same signals as their base. They need no extra LLM calls; the LLM verdict of the base signal applies. The same review dates and rules as the tournament apply. No variant replaces a frozen original during the tournament.
